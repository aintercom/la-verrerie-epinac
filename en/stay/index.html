Two guest rooms in Épinac

In an 18th-century house set in 9,000 m² of enclosed parkland, twenty minutes from Autun and thirty-five from Beaune.

The rooms

They take their names from the glass once blown here. Both have a private bathroom, a view over the park and fibre Wi-Fi. Breakfast is included.
