A select few

Food & markets

Six restaurants and five markets that we go to ourselves. Not a list: a selection.

  • I.

    Le Charlemagne

    One Michelin star · Pernand-Vergelesses

    Looking straight out over the Corton vineyards. Seasonal Franco-Japanese cooking by chef Laurent Peugeot. Lunch menu at €90, à la carte €130. Our choice for special occasions.

    Visit →
    45 min38 km by road
  • II.

    L'Hostellerie de Levernois

    One Michelin star · Levernois

    A discreet country-house hotel near Beaune, serving flawlessly executed classic cuisine. Set menu €90, à la carte €140. Book 3 weeks ahead.

    Visit →
    40 min37 km by road
  • III.

    Loiseau des Vignes

    Smart brasserie · Beaune

    Bernard Loiseau's restaurant in the heart of Beaune. Carefully made bistro cooking, a remarkable wine list, sensible prices. Lunch menu €38, dinner €65. Ideal for lunch after visiting the cellars.

    35 min33 km by road
  • IV.

    Le Chapitre

    Burgundian cooking · Autun

    Opposite the cathedral, a serious restaurant serving regional cooking with a fresh touch. Œufs en meurette, guinea fowl with morels, Charolais beef. Lunch menu €32, dinner €55. Our personal favourite.

    20 min19 km by road
  • V.

    L'Auberge de la Loue

    Regional cooking · Épinac

    Five minutes from the house, the village inn where you eat what the region provides. €30 menu (starter, main, dessert), wine by the carafe. Unpretentious, but spot on.

    5 minfrom Épinac
  • VI.

    Restaurant des Princes

    Château hotel · Couches

    Inside the Château de Couches, seasonal fine dining. Set menu €65, vaulted dining room. A lovely alternative for dinner just 20 min away, without going all the way to Beaune.

    20 min19 km by road
  • VII.

    Marché d'Autun

    Farmers' market · Wednesday & Friday mornings

    One of the finest markets in the département: Cîteaux cheeses, Morvan charcuterie, vegetables from local market gardeners. Place du Champ-de-Mars, 8am–noon.

    20 min19 km by road
  • VIII.

    Marché de Beaune

    Food market · Saturday mornings

    The big Saturday market on Place de la Halle: Burgundy producers, cheesemongers, winegrowers. One of the highlights of the week in the wine capital.

    35 min33 km by road
  • IX.

    Marché de Couches

    Small village market · Sunday mornings

    The authentic market of a village of 1,700 people: local producers, a human scale, a peaceful morning atmosphere.

    20 min19 km by road
  • X.

    Marché bio de Saulieu

    Organic producers · Wednesday mornings

    A quality market of organic producers in the heart of Saulieu: market gardeners, goat's cheese makers, Morvan honeys, sourdough breads. Combine it with a visit to the basilica.

    45 min46 km by road
